# Sample Narration Script

This is a placeholder script. Replace with your own narration, then generate
TTS audio (podcast_audio.wav) and subtitles (podcast_audio.srt) from this
script. The section names in [SECTION:xxx] markers MUST match the `name`
fields in public/timing.json — that's how Video.js maps a section to its
visual case.

Estimate ~150 English words per minute (~280 Chinese chars per minute).
A 75-second video needs roughly ~190 English words / ~350 Chinese chars.

---

[SECTION:hero]
Welcome — in the next sixty seconds, you'll learn the five patterns that make
explainer videos work.

[SECTION:overview]
We'll cover three things: the hook, the body, and the takeaway. Each one
has a job; miss any of them and the video sags.

[SECTION:stat]
Seventy-three percent of viewers finish videos under ninety seconds. Length
is a feature, not a bug.

[SECTION:feature]
The deep dive is where you earn trust. Pick one feature, show it working,
resist the urge to list everything else.

[SECTION:comparison]
Side-by-side framing works because it lets the viewer decide. Show the
before, show the after, let the contrast do the persuading.

[SECTION:timeline]
Stories move in one direction. Pick a start, pick an end, and only include
the milestones that move the narrative forward.

[SECTION:flow]
Process shots demystify. Input, transform, output — three boxes and two
arrows is enough for most explainer videos.

[SECTION:quote]
As the saying goes: design is not just what it looks like — design is how
it works.

[SECTION:data]
When you show numbers, anchor them. Eighty-two, sixty-four, forty-seven,
twenty-eight — each one needs a label, not just a bar.

[SECTION:metrics]
Key metrics belong together. Users, uptime, reach, rating — four numbers in
one frame is the most a viewer can hold.

[SECTION:steps]
Tutorials work in threes. Sign up, connect, publish — three steps is the
maximum a viewer will follow without rewatching.

[SECTION:diagram]
Architecture diagrams work when they're simple. Three nodes, two arrows,
one sentence per node.

[SECTION:summary]
So that's the pattern. Hook, body, takeaway. Everything else is decoration.

[SECTION:outro]
Thanks for watching. Like, save, subscribe — and I'll see you next time.
